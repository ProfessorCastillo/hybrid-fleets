## Implementation assumptions requiring reconciliation

- 0.986×20.3 + 0.014×35.1 = **20.5072**, not the PRD’s 20.5052. Both round to 20.5.
- 2.8 + 17.6 = 20.4, the displayed total is 20.3; the app derives labor as 2.7. These printed rounded values do not identify the underlying precision used in the study.
- Capacity weighting the default five tiers yields 302.2438, not 322.1. The tabulated common mean is retained explicitly.
- Under the app’s selected supply-mixture convention, rho=1 raises mean total crowd supply to 1610.5. Confirming the convention mapping remains part of reproducing the experiment.
- E_B[argmin_Q E_D G] and argmin_Q E_B,D G are distinct. The fixed cost curve checks the second; conditional paper sweeps report the first.
- The PRD’s proposed fixed-v split would be unbounded in the current app objective. The app instead assumes a proportional split for its extension; the published experiment’s split has not been established.
